What to Wear to Your First Lagree Class: A Women’s Outfit Guide

Your first Lagree class may look familiar if you have tried Pilates, but the pace, equipment, and time under tension can feel different. You do not need a brand-new wardrobe. You need clothes that let you move slowly, stay comfortable on a machine, and focus on the instructor.

A recent report on Yelp search interest put Lagree in the spotlight. Search activity is a useful prompt to explore a class, not proof that it suits everyone. Use this guide to prepare an outfit that works for your body and your studio’s rules.

Know the Class Format

Lagree classes commonly use a spring-loaded machine and slow, controlled transitions. Your local studio decides the exact format, level, and safety instructions. Tell the instructor it is your first class, arrive early enough for an equipment introduction, and take the modification offered when you need it.

Choose a Bra You Can Move In

Start with the support level that feels comfortable for controlled, low-impact movement. Raise your arms, lean forward, and hold a gentle plank position before class. Check the band, straps, and coverage. If your studio mixes in jumps or other impact, reassess support for those specific movements rather than relying on a generic “studio” label.

Check Leggings on the Machine

Full-length or 7/8 leggings can keep fabric between your legs and the carriage or straps, but your studio may have its own clothing guidance. In a mirror, try a comfortable squat, side lunge, and plank. Look for coverage, a waistband you do not need to pull up, and seams that feel smooth when you bend. Compression should feel supportive to you, never restrictive.

Ask About Grip Socks

Many Lagree studios require grip socks for traction and hygiene. Check your booking confirmation before you go; some studios sell or lend them, while others expect you to bring a pair. Choose socks that stay on your feet and follow the instructor’s placement guidance. Regular slippery socks are not a substitute.

Do a Five-Minute Fit Check

Pack water, a small towel if your studio suggests one, and a light layer for the trip home. Before leaving, try this short check:

  1. Squat and hinge slowly: can you breathe and move freely?
  2. Lean forward: does your bra stay comfortable and covered?
  3. Step sideways: do the leggings stay put without catching?
  4. Remove anything loose from pockets, wrists, or neckline that could meet the machine.
  5. Confirm the studio’s grip-sock rule and arrival time.

A Note from Fanka: Fit is personal. A piece you already own may be your best first-class choice. If you want to try compression activewear, test it through the actual class movements and review the garment’s current fit details.

FAQ

Can I wear ordinary leggings to Lagree?
Yes, if they meet your studio’s rules and stay comfortable through the movements. Check fabric coverage and whether hems or pockets might catch.
Are compression leggings required?
No. Choose the fit you prefer. Everyday compression activewear is not a treatment or a requirement for the class.
Do I need grip socks?
Ask your studio. Many require them, but policies vary. Follow their instructions for safe use of the equipment.
What if a movement hurts?
Stop and tell your instructor. Medical concerns or persistent pain should be discussed with a healthcare professional.
