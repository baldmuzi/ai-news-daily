What to Wear for Padel: A Women’s Court-to-Club Outfit Guide

What to Wear for Padel: A Women’s Court-to-Club Outfit Guide

By Fanka Editor | Oct 26th, 2026 | 6 Min Read
By Fanka Editor | Oct 26th, 2026 | 6 Min Read

Padel asks for quick side steps, short sprints, turns, and plenty of time with your arms in motion. An outfit should let you focus on the next ball, then feel comfortable when the match turns into coffee with your group. The right starting point is how each piece fits your body and your style of play.

The new College Padel League is one example of how the sport is gaining more organized places to play, including women’s competition. This guide is for any woman heading to a casual court, lesson, or club match.

Start With Your Court and Session

Check whether you will play indoors or outdoors, how long your session will last, and whether you are joining a class or a match. For a cool outdoor start, carry a removable top layer. For a warm indoor court, prioritize a breathable base. Court shoes with suitable grip matter more than a fashionable street sneaker; ask your club what surface you will use.

Choose Sports Bra Support for Your Movement

Reach overhead, rotate your shoulders, and take several side steps before choosing a sports bra. The band should stay in place and allow a comfortable breath. A light-support bra tank may suit some relaxed sessions; a faster game with repeated starts and stops may call for more support. Match the bra to your own movement and fit rather than assuming one design works for every player.

Check Bottoms, Waistband, and Pockets

Leggings, capris, and shorts can all work on court. Test lateral lunges and a low ready position: the waistband should stay put without folding, and the fabric should not pull at the knee or inner thigh. If you need a ball pocket, check that it holds a ball securely without bouncing or restricting your stride. A phone pocket is useful after play but may not be the right shape for a padel ball.

A Five-Move Fit Test Before You Go

Try five familiar motions at home: reach high, rotate, side step, take a short lunge, and sit. Notice straps, waistband, opacity, and any rubbing. Repeat with the layer you plan to wear after the match. This takes less time than discovering a distracting seam during your first rally.

FAQ

Can I wear leggings for padel?
Yes, if they allow quick lateral movement and stay comfortable through your session. Try the fit test before match day.
Do I need a special pocket?
Only if your play style needs one. A phone pocket and a ball pocket serve different purposes, so test what you carry.
What should I wear after a match?
Carry an easy outer layer and change out of damp clothes when practical. Choose comfort for the weather and your next stop.

Final Thoughts

Build your padel outfit around movement first: personal bra support, bottoms that pass a side-step test, suitable shoes, and a layer for the journey home. Keep the pieces that let you enjoy the game and your community.
