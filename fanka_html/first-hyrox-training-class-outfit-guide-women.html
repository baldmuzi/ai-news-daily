What to Wear to Your First HYROX Training Class: A Women’s Outfit Guide

Your first HYROX training class may move between running, strength, and functional stations. That mix makes one simple question surprisingly useful: can you move freely without adjusting your outfit at every transition?

A new EGYM and HYROX training partnership brings structured class content into gym member apps. The announcement is news; the fit checks below are a lasting guide you can use for any hybrid training session.

Know the Class Format Before You Dress

Ask your coach whether the session includes sustained running, sled work, lunges, carries, or a shorter skills practice. Class plans vary. Pack for the actual workout, the room temperature, and how you will get home afterward.

Choose Sports Bra Support for the Highest-Impact Part

Running usually sets the support requirement, even when the class also includes strength work. Try a few short jogs and arm movements in the bra you plan to wear. The fit should feel secure without painful pressure or restricted breathing. Fanka’s Body Sculpt Sports Bra is described as light support, so choose another support level if your running test calls for it.

Check Leggings Fit Through Transitions

Look for a waistband that stays put when you squat, bend, and move between stations. Check opacity in your own lighting and make sure seams do not rub during a short run. Compression can be a personal preference for fit and feel; it is not a substitute for training progression or recovery.

Plan Shoes, Socks, and Layers Together

A mixed class asks for footwear you can run and train in comfortably. Ask the coach about the floor and station requirements, then test your shoes with the same socks you will wear. Bring a removable layer for the trip to and from class, especially in colder weather. Avoid carrying a phone in a pocket if it shifts during running or catches on equipment.

Do a Five-Minute Outfit Trial Run

Before class, jog briefly, squat, lunge, hinge, and lift your arms. Notice any slipping, pinching, rubbing, or distraction. If something needs constant adjustment, change one piece before the session. Your first class is for learning the movements, not testing how much discomfort you can ignore.

FAQ

Do I need special HYROX clothing for my first class?
No. Start with comfortable sportswear that passes a running and movement test, and follow your coach’s equipment guidance.
Are compression leggings required?
No. They are one fit option. Prioritize a waistband, coverage, and seams that feel comfortable during the class.
Should I wear a light-support sports bra?
Choose support for the highest-impact movement in your session. A light-support style may suit warm-ups or gentler work, but many runners prefer more support.
