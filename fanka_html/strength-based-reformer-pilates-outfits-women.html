What to Wear for Strength-Based Reformer Pilates: A Women’s Guide

By Fanka Editor | Sep 11th, 2026 | 5 Min Read

By Fanka Editor | Sep 11th, 2026 | 5 Min Read

Reformer Pilates is no longer limited to slow, stretch-focused sessions. New class formats are adding dedicated upper-body, lower-body and core strength work while keeping the reformer’s controlled resistance and precise movement.

That mix changes what you need from an outfit. You want fabric that moves with a long lunge, a waistband that stays comfortable during core work and enough support to transition from the carriage to standing strength exercises without constant adjustments.

Understand the new strength-Pilates mix

A strength-based reformer class may combine springs, straps, planks, lunges, rows, bridges and targeted upper- or lower-body blocks. The format is still low-impact in the sense that it is controlled and joint-conscious, but it can feel demanding because you are working against resistance while changing position.

Choose a stable base layer

Start with leggings or capris that feel secure but not restrictive. A high-rise waistband can help you feel covered during bridges and forward folds, while a smooth, flexible fabric makes repeated transitions less distracting. Match support to the movement

Choose a bra or tank based on your own body and the class intensity rather than a generic label. For upper-body sequences, look for straps that stay in place when you reach and rotate. For lower-body strength work, make sure the top of the leggings remains comfortable when you brace, breathe and hinge.

A good fit check is simple: squat, reach overhead, take a deep breath and sit on the floor. If you are tugging at the waistband or adjusting the straps before you have even started, try a different size, cut or support level.

Style the hours after class

Strength-based Pilates often sits inside a full day. Add an oversized button-down, cropped jacket or light knit over the same base, then change your shoes for the commute or coffee stop. The goal is not to hide the workout outfit—it is to make the transition feel intentional.

FAQ

Are compression leggings suitable for reformer Pilates?

They can be, if the fit feels comfortable and does not restrict breathing, joint movement or your ability to notice body position. Choose based on personal preference and the class.

Should I wear shorts or leggings?

Leggings offer coverage and can make carriage work feel simpler. Shorts may feel cooler, but choose a length and fabric that stays comfortable during straps, bridges and floor transitions.

What should I wear for a Pilates class with strength blocks?

Try a stable high-rise base, a supportive bra or tank and a light layer for before and after class. Test the full range of motion at home before your first session.
